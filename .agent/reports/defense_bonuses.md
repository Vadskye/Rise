# Analysis of Non-Enhancement Defense Bonuses Gained at Rank 3+

This report lists and analyzes all class abilities, species features, cleric domains, and feats in *Rise* that meet the following criteria:
1. They provide a bonus to one or more defenses.
2. The bonus is **not** an "enhancement bonus".
3. The ability is first accessible at **rank 3 or higher** (for class archetype abilities, this is `rank >= 3`; for feats, this means character level 7+, corresponding to character rank 3+).

---

## I. Class Archetype & Species Abilities

### 1. Barbarian
* **Titanic Rage** (*Battlerager, Rank 7*): Size category increases up to Huge; grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Brawn defense** (also incurs a `\minus1` penalty to Reflex defense).

### 2. Cleric
* **Experienced Spellcaster** (*DivineSpellMastery, Rank 7*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Mental defense**.
* **Healer's Grace+** (*Healer, Rank 5*): Increases the defense bonus from Healer's Grace to **`\plus2` to all defenses** (base version is level 2 / Rank 1).

### 3. Druid
* **Elemental Balance+** (*Elementalist, Rank 7*): Earth element grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Brawn defense** (base version gives `\plus1` Fortitude defense at Rank 2).
* **Experienced Spellcaster** (*NatureSpellMastery, Rank 7*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Mental defense**.
* **Transformative Toughness** (*Shifter, Rank 6*): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to Fortitude defense**.

### 4. Fighter
* **Armored Core** (*EquipmentTraining, Rank 4*): While wearing body armor, you gain a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
* **Armor Expertise+** (*EquipmentTraining, Rank 7*): Medium armor grants a **`\plus2` bonus to Brawn, Fortitude, and Reflex defenses** (up to a maximum equal to your Armor defense).
* **Specialized Bulwark** (*Sentinel, Rank 5*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense and one other defense** of your choice.
* **Shifting Stance** (*Tactician, Rank 4*): Defense stance grants a **`\plus1` bonus to all defenses**.

### 5. Monk
* **Unhindered Agility+** (*PerfectedForm, Rank 7*): Increases the defense bonus of Unhindered Agility (Armor and Reflex) to **`\plus2`** (base version is Rank 1).

### 6. Paladin
* **Experienced Spellcaster** (*DivineSpellExpertise, Rank 7*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
* **Stalwart Defense** (*StalwartGuardian, Rank 3*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor and Fortitude defenses**.

### 7. Ranger
* **Know Your Enemy+** (*BoundaryWarden, Rank 7*): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to all defenses** against creatures associated with trained Knowledge skills.

### 8. Rogue
* **Well Rounded** (*JackOfAllTrades, Rank 6*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to all defenses** that are currently lower than your highest defense.
* **Slippery Mind** (*SuaveScoundrel, Rank 4*): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to Mental defense**.

### 9. Sorcerer
* **Experienced Spellcaster** (*SorcererArcaneSpellMastery, Rank 7*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Fortitude defense**.
* **Mystic Supremacy** (*InnateArcanist, Rank 7*): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to defenses against magical attacks**.

### 10. Votive
* **Sacrificial Covenant++** (*CovenantKeeper, Rank 7*): Under Covenant of Bloodforging, increases the Armor defense bonus to **`\plus5`** (base carapace is Rank 1, providing `\plus4`).
* **Calculating+** (*Soulforged [Devil], Rank 7*): Grants a **bonus to Mental defense equal to half your Intelligence** (base version at Rank 4 only grants power bonuses).

### 11. Wizard
* **Experienced Spellcaster** (*WizardArcaneSpellMastery, Rank 7*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Mental defense**.
* **Alchemical Tolerance** (*Alchemist, Rank 3*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Fortitude defense**.
* **School Specialization+** (*SchoolSpecialist, Rank 4*): Under Transmutation school, increases the defense bonus to **`\plus3`** (base version is Rank 1, providing `\plus2` to a chosen defense).
* **School Specialization++** (*SchoolSpecialist, Rank 7*): 
  * Under Abjuration, increases the Armor/Reflex defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (base version is Rank 1, providing `\plus1`).
  * Under Transmutation, increases the chosen defense bonus to **`\plus4`** (base version is Rank 1, providing `\plus2`).

### 12. Uncommon Species
* **Draconic Bulk** (*Dragon, Rank 3*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
* **Winged Agility+** (*Harpy, Rank 3*): Increases the Armor defense bonus of Winged Agility to **`\plus3`** (base species version is level 1, providing `\plus2`).
* **Bonded Boon** (*Naiad, Rank 3*): River or stream bond grants a **`\plus2` bonus to Reflex defense**.
* **Amorphous Form+** (*Oozeborn, Rank 3*): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to defenses** when determining whether an attack gets a critical hit.
* **Amorphous Form++** (*Oozeborn, Rank 6*): Increases the critical defense bonus to **`\plus4`**.
* **Unholy Resilience** (*Vampire, Rank 5*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor, Brawn, Reflex, and Mental defenses** (takes a `\minus2` penalty to Fortitude).

---

## II. Cleric Domain Abilities (Granted via *Domain Influence* archetype)

* **Protection Domain - Essence** (*Domain Influence Rank 4*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
* **Forge Domain - Mastery** (*Domain Influence Rank 7*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
* **Knowledge Domain - Mastery** (*Domain Influence Rank 7*): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Brawn, Fortitude, Mental, and Reflex defenses**.

---

## III. Feat Abilities (Level 7+ / Rank 3+)

### 1. Feat Features (First accessible at Level 7+)
* **Ancestral Protectors** (*Barbaric Ancestry, Level 12 / Rank 4*): While at least one spirit is active, you gain a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
* **Defensive Stance** (*Duelist, Level 12 / Rank 4*): While wielding a melee weapon in at least one hand, you gain a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense** (doubled to **`\plus2`** if not using a shield).
* **Made of Iron** (*Living Weapon, Level 12 / Rank 4*): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to defenses** when determining whether an attack gets a critical hit.
* **Made of Iron+** (*Living Weapon, Level 18 / Rank 6*): Increases the critical defense bonus to **`\plus4`**.
* **Malleable Flesh** (*Sphere Focus: Polymorph, Level 18 / Rank 6*): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to defenses** when determining whether an attack gets a critical hit.
* **Evasive Shade** (*Sphere Focus: Umbramancy, Level 18 / Rank 6*): If you have the evasion monk or rogue ability, you gain a **`\plus2` bonus to Armor and Reflex defenses against area attacks**.
* **Eye of the Storm** (*Whirlwind Warrior, Level 12 / Rank 4*): You gain a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor and Reflex defenses** against creatures sharing space.

### 2. Feat Feature Upgrades (Base ability at Level 1 or 6, upgrade at Level 12+)
* **Simple Life+ & ++** (*Ascetic, Level 12 & 18*): Increases the non-Armor defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (Level 12) and **`\plus3`** (Level 18). Base ability is Level 6.
* **Staggering Brawn+** (*Beef, Level 12 / Rank 4*): Increases the Brawn defense bonus to **`\plus6`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus3`).
* **Brawling Expertise+** (*Brawler, Level 12 / Rank 4*): Increases the Brawn defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus1`).
* **Scales+** (*Draconic Ancestry, Level 18 / Rank 6*): Increases the Armor defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus1`).
* **Duel Focus+** (*Duelist, Level 18 / Rank 6*): Increases the defense bonus vs. a chosen target to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus1`).
* **Mental Discipline+** (*Iron Will, Level 12 / Rank 4*): Increases the Mental defense bonus to **`\plus6`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus3`).
* **Studied Defense+** (*Knowledge Specialization, Level 18 / Rank 6*): Increases the chosen defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 6, providing `\plus1`).
* **Mundane Resilience+ & ++** (*Null, Level 12 & 18*): Increases the non-Armor defenses bonus to **`\plus2`** (Level 12) and **`\plus3`** (Level 18). Base is Level 6.
* **Lightning Reflexes+** (*Rapid Reaction, Level 12 / Rank 4*): Increases the Reflex defense bonus to **`\plus6`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus3`).
* **Shield Expertise+** (*Shieldbearer, Level 12 / Rank 4*): Increases the Armor defense bonus while wielding a shield to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus1`).
* **Defensive Barriers+** (*Sphere Focus: Verdamancy, Level 18 / Rank 6*): Increases the Armor defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 6, providing `\plus1`).
