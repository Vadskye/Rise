# Analysis of Non-Enhancement Defense Bonuses Gained at Rank 3+

This report lists and analyzes all class abilities, species features, cleric domains, and feats in _Rise_ that meet the following criteria:

1. They provide a bonus to one or more defenses.
2. The bonus is **not** an "enhancement bonus".
3. The ability is first accessible at **rank 3 or higher** (for class archetype abilities, this is `rank >= 3`; for feats, this means character level 7+, corresponding to character rank 3+).

---

## I. Class Archetype & Species Abilities

### 1. Barbarian

- **Titanic Rage** (_Battlerager, Rank 7_): Size category increases up to Huge; grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Brawn defense** (also incurs a `\minus1` penalty to Reflex defense).

### 2. Cleric

- **Experienced Spellcaster** (_DivineSpellMastery, Rank 7_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Mental defense**.
- **Healer's Grace+** (_Healer, Rank 5_): Increases the defense bonus from Healer's Grace to **`\plus2` to all defenses** (base version is level 2 / Rank 1).

### 3. Druid

- **Elemental Balance+** (_Elementalist, Rank 7_): Earth element grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Brawn defense** (base version gives `\plus1` Fortitude defense at Rank 2).
- **Experienced Spellcaster** (_NatureSpellMastery, Rank 7_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Mental defense**.
- **Transformative Toughness** (_Shifter, Rank 6_): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to Fortitude defense**.

### 4. Fighter

- **Armored Core** (_EquipmentTraining, Rank 4_): While wearing body armor, you gain a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
- **Armor Expertise+** (_EquipmentTraining, Rank 7_): Medium armor grants a **`\plus2` bonus to Brawn, Fortitude, and Reflex defenses** (up to a maximum equal to your Armor defense).
- **Specialized Bulwark** (_Sentinel, Rank 5_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense and one other defense** of your choice.
- **Shifting Stance** (_Tactician, Rank 4_): Defense stance grants a **`\plus1` bonus to all defenses**.

### 5. Monk

- **Unhindered Agility+** (_PerfectedForm, Rank 7_): Increases the defense bonus of Unhindered Agility (Armor and Reflex) to **`\plus2`** (base version is Rank 1).

### 6. Paladin

- **Experienced Spellcaster** (_DivineSpellExpertise, Rank 7_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
- **Stalwart Defense** (_StalwartGuardian, Rank 3_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor and Fortitude defenses**.

### 7. Ranger

- **Know Your Enemy+** (_BoundaryWarden, Rank 7_): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to all defenses** against creatures associated with trained Knowledge skills.

### 8. Rogue

- **Well Rounded** (_JackOfAllTrades, Rank 6_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to all defenses** that are currently lower than your highest defense.
- **Slippery Mind** (_SuaveScoundrel, Rank 4_): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to Mental defense**.

### 9. Sorcerer

- **Experienced Spellcaster** (_ArcaneSpellIntuition, Rank 7_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Fortitude defense**.
- **Mystic Supremacy** (_InnateArcanist, Rank 7_): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to defenses against magical attacks**.

### 10. Votive

- **Sacrificial Covenant++** (_CovenantKeeper, Rank 7_): Under Covenant of Bloodforging, increases the Armor defense bonus to **`\plus5`** (base carapace is Rank 1, providing `\plus4`).
- **Calculating+** (_Soulforged [Devil], Rank 7_): Grants a **bonus to Mental defense equal to half your Intelligence** (base version at Rank 4 only grants power bonuses).

### 11. Wizard

- **Experienced Spellcaster** (_ArcaneSpellMastery, Rank 7_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Mental defense**.
- **Alchemical Tolerance** (_Alchemist, Rank 3_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Fortitude defense**.
- **School Specialization+** (_SchoolSpecialist, Rank 4_): Under Transmutation school, increases the defense bonus to **`\plus3`** (base version is Rank 1, providing `\plus2` to a chosen defense).
- **School Specialization++** (_SchoolSpecialist, Rank 7_):
  - Under Abjuration, increases the Armor/Reflex defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (base version is Rank 1, providing `\plus1`).
  - Under Transmutation, increases the chosen defense bonus to **`\plus4`** (base version is Rank 1, providing `\plus2`).

### 12. Uncommon Species

- **Draconic Bulk** (_Dragon, Rank 3_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
- **Winged Agility+** (_Harpy, Rank 3_): Increases the Armor defense bonus of Winged Agility to **`\plus3`** (base species version is level 1, providing `\plus2`).
- **Bonded Boon** (_Naiad, Rank 3_): River or stream bond grants a **`\plus2` bonus to Reflex defense**.
- **Amorphous Form+** (_Oozeborn, Rank 3_): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to defenses** when determining whether an attack gets a critical hit.
- **Amorphous Form++** (_Oozeborn, Rank 6_): Increases the critical defense bonus to **`\plus4`**.
- **Unholy Resilience** (_Vampire, Rank 5_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor, Brawn, Reflex, and Mental defenses** (takes a `\minus2` penalty to Fortitude).

---

## II. Cleric Domain Abilities (Granted via _Domain Influence_ archetype)

- **Protection Domain - Essence** (_Domain Influence Rank 4_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
- **Forge Domain - Mastery** (_Domain Influence Rank 7_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
- **Knowledge Domain - Mastery** (_Domain Influence Rank 7_): Grants a **`\plus1` bonus to Brawn, Fortitude, Mental, and Reflex defenses**.

---

## III. Feat Abilities (Level 7+ / Rank 3+)

### 1. Feat Features (First accessible at Level 7+)

- **Ancestral Protectors** (_Barbaric Ancestry, Level 12 / Rank 4_): While at least one spirit is active, you gain a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense**.
- **Defensive Stance** (_Duelist, Level 12 / Rank 4_): While wielding a melee weapon in at least one hand, you gain a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor defense** (doubled to **`\plus2`** if not using a shield).
- **Made of Iron** (_Living Weapon, Level 12 / Rank 4_): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to defenses** when determining whether an attack gets a critical hit.
- **Made of Iron+** (_Living Weapon, Level 18 / Rank 6_): Increases the critical defense bonus to **`\plus4`**.
- **Malleable Flesh** (_Sphere Focus: Polymorph, Level 18 / Rank 6_): Grants a **`\plus2` bonus to defenses** when determining whether an attack gets a critical hit.
- **Evasive Shade** (_Sphere Focus: Umbramancy, Level 18 / Rank 6_): If you have the evasion monk or rogue ability, you gain a **`\plus2` bonus to Armor and Reflex defenses against area attacks**.
- **Eye of the Storm** (_Whirlwind Warrior, Level 12 / Rank 4_): You gain a **`\plus1` bonus to Armor and Reflex defenses** against creatures sharing space.

### 2. Feat Feature Upgrades (Base ability at Level 1 or 6, upgrade at Level 12+)

- **Simple Life+ & ++** (_Ascetic, Level 12 & 18_): Increases the non-Armor defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (Level 12) and **`\plus3`** (Level 18). Base ability is Level 6.
- **Staggering Brawn+** (_Beef, Level 12 / Rank 4_): Increases the Brawn defense bonus to **`\plus6`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus3`).
- **Brawling Expertise+** (_Brawler, Level 12 / Rank 4_): Increases the Brawn defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus1`).
- **Scales+** (_Draconic Ancestry, Level 18 / Rank 6_): Increases the Armor defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus1`).
- **Duel Focus+** (_Duelist, Level 18 / Rank 6_): Increases the defense bonus vs. a chosen target to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus1`).
- **Mental Discipline+** (_Iron Will, Level 12 / Rank 4_): Increases the Mental defense bonus to **`\plus6`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus3`).
- **Studied Defense+** (_Knowledge Specialization, Level 18 / Rank 6_): Increases the chosen defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 6, providing `\plus1`).
- **Mundane Resilience+ & ++** (_Null, Level 12 & 18_): Increases the non-Armor defenses bonus to **`\plus2`** (Level 12) and **`\plus3`** (Level 18). Base is Level 6.
- **Lightning Reflexes+** (_Rapid Reaction, Level 12 / Rank 4_): Increases the Reflex defense bonus to **`\plus6`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus3`).
- **Shield Expertise+** (_Shieldbearer, Level 12 / Rank 4_): Increases the Armor defense bonus while wielding a shield to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 1, providing `\plus1`).
- **Defensive Barriers+** (_Sphere Focus: Verdamancy, Level 18 / Rank 6_): Increases the Armor defense bonus to **`\plus2`** (base is Level 6, providing `\plus1`).
